Smart Scanner Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Smart Scanner is a document scanning, OCR, and PDF management application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use the application.

1. Information We Process

Smart Scanner may process documents, photographs, PDF files, and text that you choose to scan, import, or create using the application.

Most document processing features are performed locally on your device.

2. Document Scanning and Local Storage

Documents scanned or imported into Smart Scanner are stored locally on your device unless you explicitly use a feature that requires an online service.

Smart Scanner may store:

  • Scanned document images
  • Generated PDF files
  • Document names and categories
  • OCR text extracted from documents
  • Application preferences and settings

You can delete documents from the application at any time.

3. Offline OCR

Smart Scanner provides offline Optical Character Recognition (OCR) for supported languages, including Arabic and English.

When Offline OCR is used, document images are processed locally on your device and are not sent to an external AI service for OCR processing.

4. AI OCR

Smart Scanner may provide an optional AI OCR feature for higher-accuracy text recognition.

When you choose to use AI OCR, selected document pages or images may be securely transmitted over the Internet to Smart Scanner's processing service and an external artificial intelligence service provider for the purpose of extracting text from the document.

AI OCR is optional. Users may continue to use Offline OCR without using the online AI OCR feature.

Document content submitted for AI OCR is used for providing the requested OCR functionality and is not intentionally used by Smart Scanner for advertising. Processing by third-party AI providers may also be governed by their own privacy policies and service terms.

5. Camera and Photos

Smart Scanner may request access to your device camera in order to scan documents.

The application may also request access to photos or files when you choose to import an existing document or image.

These permissions are used only to provide document scanning, importing, editing, OCR, and PDF functionality.

6. Advertising

The free version of Smart Scanner may display advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may collect information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, application interaction data, and diagnostic information as permitted by applicable laws and your device settings.

For more information about how Google processes information, please visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. In-App Purchases

Smart Scanner may offer paid features, including Pro features and AI OCR credits. Payments are processed through Google Play Billing.

Smart Scanner does not directly receive or store your full payment card details. Payment information is processed by Google Play according to Google's policies.

8. Analytics and Technical Information

Third-party services integrated into the application may process limited technical information required for their operation, security, fraud prevention, billing, advertising, or diagnostics.

This information may include:

  • Device type and operating system
  • Application version
  • IP address
  • Advertising or device identifiers where permitted
  • Crash and diagnostic information

9. Data Sharing

Smart Scanner does not sell users' scanned documents or extracted text.

Information may be shared only when necessary to provide specific services requested by the user, including:

  • AI OCR processing providers
  • Google AdMob for advertising
  • Google Play for purchases and licensing

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect the security and integrity of the application.

10. Data Retention

Documents stored locally remain on your device until you delete them, uninstall the application, or remove application data.

For online AI OCR, data may be temporarily processed by service providers as required to complete the OCR request. Retention practices of external providers are governed by their applicable policies and agreements.

11. Data Security

Smart Scanner uses reasonable technical measures to protect data handled by the application.

Online communications used by AI OCR and other network services are intended to use secure HTTPS connections.

However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

12. Children's Privacy

Smart Scanner is a general productivity application and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly through the application.

13. User Choices

Users may:

  • Use Offline OCR instead of AI OCR
  • Delete scanned documents stored in the application
  • Control camera and storage permissions through Android settings
  • Uninstall the application and remove locally stored application data
  • Control advertising preferences using applicable Google and Android settings

14. Third-Party Services

Smart Scanner may use third-party services including:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob
  • Artificial intelligence/OCR processing services when AI OCR is selected

Use of these services may be subject to the respective providers' privacy policies.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Smart Scanner adds or changes features, services, or legal requirements.

The latest version will be made available at this web page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly.
